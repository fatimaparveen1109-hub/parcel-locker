How It Works

1. Order

Pick products and choose your preferred locker hub.

2. Deliver

A rider takes your parcel to the hub and drops it in a locker.

3. Collect

Scan your private QR code and collect within 48 hours.

Privacy Policy

We share only what each person needs. Sellers see your name and order items. Riders see only the delivery ID, parcel size and hub. Your phone and address stay private. Payments in this demo are simulated; no real money moves.
